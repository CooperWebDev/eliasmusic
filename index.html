Everything you need to practise

Two ways to play

Use the on screen keys, or turn on the microphone and play your own piano. No account and no downloads.

Falling notes or sheet music

Switch between a piano roll and a treble clef staff at any time while you learn the same song.

Instant, clear feedback

Green for right, red for wrong, with a live accuracy score and streak so you can see yourself improve.

How a session works

01

Pick a song

Start with a beginner favourite from the library, or type your own notes.

02

Choose a mode

Learning mode waits for you. Practice mode keeps time and scores your timing.

03

Slow it down, loop it

Set the tempo from 50% to 125% and repeat a few measures until they feel easy.
