About Elia’s Music

Elia’s Music is a small, free practice tool for people learning piano. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded and there is no signup.

Practice modes

Learning pauses the song at each note until you play the right key. Practice keeps a steady tempo and scores your timing and accuracy.

Playing on a real piano

Turn on the microphone and play. The app listens for one note at a time, so chords are not detected. A quiet room and a phone or laptop close to the piano give the best results.

MIDI keyboards

Plug in a USB MIDI keyboard and allow access when asked. MIDI works in Chrome and Edge on desktop and Android. Safari on iPhone does not support it.

Questions

Why won’t the microphone start? Browsers only allow it on secure (https) pages or on localhost, and only after you grant permission.

Which notes are on screen? Two octaves, C4 to B5. Computer key shortcuts cover C4 to F5.
